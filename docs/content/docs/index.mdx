title: App AI Gateway
description: Give your apps access to AI providers without shipping provider keys, and see every request in one place.
---

App AI Gateway sits between your apps and the AI providers they use. Your app
calls the gateway, and the gateway calls OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini or whichever
provider you added. Your provider keys stay on the gateway. Every request is
checked before it goes out. Every provider call is recorded with its cost, so
you can see where the money went.

![Your iOS app calls App AI Gateway, which holds the provider keys, applies per-user limits, records usage and cost, and forwards the request to the provider.](/overview.png)

It is built for iOS apps first. The app talks to the gateway directly, and the
gateway checks that the request comes from a real install of your app, from a
signed-in user, and, where you require it, from a user who has paid. Server
applications are supported too, with a secret app key, so you can watch all your
apps from one console.

## What you get

- **Provider keys stay off devices.** A key is encrypted the moment you add it
  and is never shown again. Your app never holds one.
- **One gateway for all your apps.** Add an app in the console instead of
  building a backend for each one.
- **Requests are checked before they reach a provider.** Apple App Attest,
  sign-in tokens from Firebase, Supabase, Auth0, Clerk or your own issuer, and
  a paid-user check.
- **Each app calls only what you allow.** You choose the providers, operations
  and models each app may use.
- **Limits you set.** Requests per minute and per day, and a monthly budget, for
  each user and for the whole app.
- **Provider-native requests.** The gateway forwards the provider's own request
  and response formats, streaming included. Standard SDKs work once you change
  the base URL.
- **Usage in one place.** Spend, tokens and errors for each app, model,
  provider and user. Block a user who is running up your bill.

## Get started

Sign up at [console.appaigateway.com](https://console.appaigateway.com), bring
a provider key, and follow the [Quickstart](/quickstart). Your apps call
`https://api.appaigateway.com`. There is nothing to deploy.

The guides describe the same apps, providers and APIs wherever your gateway
runs. Examples use the hosted addresses.

<Callout title="Self-hosting">
  Use your own console and API addresses in these examples. If both share one
  address, use it for both.
</Callout>
