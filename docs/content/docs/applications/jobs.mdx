---
title: Jobs
description: Submit, check and cancel long-running jobs, such as image and video generation, through the gateway.
---

Some providers run work in the background: you submit a job, then check on
it until its result is ready. fal's queue works this way for image and video
models. The gateway handles these jobs from start to end, so your app never
talks to the provider directly.

## How a job runs

1. **Submit.** Your app sends the request to the provider's queue path
   through the gateway, such as
   `POST /v2/apps/{app}/proxy/{provider}/queue/fal-ai/flux/dev`. The gateway
   checks who is calling and the app's limits, as for any request. The answer
   carries a job id that the gateway made, and links that point at the
   gateway, not at the provider.
2. **Check the status.** `GET /v2/apps/{app}/jobs/{job}/status` answers with
   the provider's own status. For fal, add `?logs=1` to get the job's logs.
3. **Read the result.** `GET /v2/apps/{app}/jobs/{job}` answers with the
   provider's result, passed on as it is.
4. **Cancel, if you need to.** `PUT /v2/apps/{app}/jobs/{job}/cancel` asks the
   provider to cancel the job.

Use the links in the gateway's answers rather than building them yourself.
A job id only works for the app and the end user that submitted it.

## Checking is free

Status checks, result reads and cancels count toward no request limit, and
they add no charge. Check as often as you need.

## What a job costs

- **When you submit,** the job is charged an estimate at once, worked out
  from the request: for an image model, the images and their size; for a
  video model priced per second, the frames over the frame rate. Your
  budgets count it right away.
- **When the result is ready,** the gateway corrects the charge to what the
  provider actually billed. This happens when your app reads the result, or
  in the background within a few minutes if it never does.
- **A failed job** costs nothing when the provider reports that it billed
  nothing for it.
- **If the provider gives no figure** the gateway can use, the estimate
  stays, and the charge is marked as an estimate.

The console's **Jobs** tab on each app shows every job, with its estimate,
the correction, the total, and whether it is settled yet. `agw app job list`
shows the same.

## Cancelling

- **A job cancelled while it is still queued** costs nothing. Its charge is
  corrected to zero once the provider confirms the cancel.
- **A job that is already running** is charged in full, by the units the
  provider billed. fal's own docs say a running job may still complete after
  a cancel.
- Both hold whether you cancel through the gateway or at the provider
  directly: the charge follows the provider's final answer.

## How long results stay readable

The provider keeps a job's result for a limited time. fal keeps results for
about an hour, and large results for less. Read the result soon after the
job finishes. The gateway reads it in the background too, so the charge
settles even if your app never reads it.

## Models that are not on our price list

A model our price list does not have works once you give it a price of your
own, on the provider connection under **Your prices** in the console, or
with `agw provider update`. For example, a flat price per job:

```json
{ "fal-ai/some-model": { "per_request": 0.02 } }
```

Or a price per unit the provider reports (fal's billed units), per image, or
per video:

```json
{ "fal-ai/other-model": { "per_unit": 0.01 } }
```

A model with no price at all is refused.
