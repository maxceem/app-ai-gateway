---
title: Conversation history
description: Keep a copy of what an app's users send and what the model answers, so you can see how your AI feature is really used and improve it. Off until you turn it on, kept as long as you choose, with the media you choose.
---

Usage shows what an app's calls cost. It cannot show whether the model
understood the person: for that you need what was said and what came back. With
conversation history on, the gateway keeps a copy of every call an app makes and
the answer it got. You can download them to review, study, or turn into test
cases.

It is off for every app until you turn it on.

## What is kept

- **Text and JSON**, as the provider received it and as it answered: messages,
  instructions, tool definitions, tool calls and their results, errors.
- **Attachments**: images, audio (a recording sent for transcription, audio in a
  request, a spoken reply) and other files, such as a PDF or a text document.
  Each is kept as its own file beside the conversations, once per day however
  often it is sent. The record names it as
  `agw-media:<YYYY-MM-DD>/media/<sha256>.<ext>` in the place its data was.
  Two kinds stay inline in the record instead, where their kind is kept: the
  pieces of a streamed recording, as they came, and the text of an attached
  plain-text document.

Each kind of attachment can be left out: **Images**, **Audio** and **Files**, all
kept unless you turn one off. Media left out is recorded as
`agw-omitted:<type>;<bytes>`: its type and size. The conversation itself, its
messages, instructions and tool calls, is always kept.

Some things are recorded by their size instead of their content:

- a body over 8 MB;
- a single text inside a body that is longer than 262,144 characters;
- bodies that would make the record larger than 1,500,000 bytes. The largest
  are left out first, until the record fits, and the rest of the call is kept.

Only calls are stored: requests to an app's allowed operations, and presets.
These are never stored, though their usage is still recorded:

- a [passthrough](/applications/provider-access#passthrough) call;
- a job, such as a fal request;
- a live session, and the request that makes its token.

A body is not kept if it holds the exact text of a credential the call used: the
provider key, a Cloudflare AI Gateway token, or the app's own credential (its app
key or gateway token). That body is replaced whole by `{"omitted":"credential"}`,
in the request or the answer, even if the provider's answer only repeats the key.
A record field that holds one, such as the user ID, is replaced by
`agw-omitted:credential`. The gateway does not look for other secrets your users
may have typed.

## Turn it on

Storage needs a deployment set up to keep it; where it is not, the switch says
so and stays off.

On the app's **Settings** tab, under **Conversation history**, switch on **Store
conversations**. Choose which media to **Keep**, and how many days to keep
everything: **Keep for (days)**, 180 unless you change it, at most 400. Click
**Save conversation history**. Calls sent from then on are stored; nothing earlier
is.

Through the API, it is the `conversations` part of the app's settings:

```json
"conversations": {
  "store": true,
  "retention_days": 180,
  "keep": { "images": true, "audio": false, "files": true }
}
```

Storing conversations means keeping what your users wrote. Say so in your app's
privacy policy, and in its App Store privacy details.

## Group calls and let users opt out

Two request headers, both optional, both removed by the gateway before the
provider:

| Header | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `X-Client-Trace-Id` | Your own ID for one piece of work, such as one chat message that took several model calls. Recorded on the charge and on the stored conversation, so you can group them and join them with your app's own data. |
| `X-Conversation-Storage: off` | Leaves this call out of storage, for a user who said no. |

The charges list filters by trace ID:
`GET /v2/admin/apps/{app}/charges?trace=<id>`.

## Download them

Conversations are stored in batches of gzipped [JSON Lines](https://jsonlines.org):
one record per line, under the UTC day the call started.

With the CLI:

```sh
agw app conversations my-app --from 2026-08-01 --to 2026-10-31 --output ./conversations --media
```

It writes the batches under `./conversations/<day>/`, and with `--media` the
media the records name, under `./conversations/<day>/media/`. Run it again to
fetch only what is new.

Through the API:

- `GET /v2/admin/apps/{app}/conversations?from=YYYY-MM-DD&to=YYYY-MM-DD` lists
  the batches, a page at a time; pass `next_cursor` back as `cursor`.
- `GET /v2/admin/apps/{app}/conversations/file?name=<name>` downloads one batch,
  or one piece of media by the name a record gives it.

## How long they are kept

Once a day, stored days older than the app's retention are deleted, with their
media.

Deleting an app makes its stored conversations unavailable at once, and they are
deleted later. Download any you want to keep before you delete the app.

A call can be missing in rare cases. Storage is a record for later reading, not
part of serving or counting the call, so a call is never refused or slowed for
it.
